PHOSPHORWATCH  -  always-on-top Twitch stream window
===================================================

No install needed.

1. Right-click the zip > "Extract All..." > Extract.
   (Don't run it from inside the zip, and keep the folder together:
    PhosphorWatch.exe needs the "_internal" folder next to it.)

2. Double-click PhosphorWatch.exe.
   If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click "More info",
   then "Run anyway". (It shows that for any small app that isn't
   signed by a big company.)

Using it:
  Type a channel name (or paste a twitch.tv link) > WATCH
  +            save the channel to your favorites list
  Sign in      bottom right of the channel screen. Sign in once and
               your subscriptions / Turbo apply, so channels you're
               subbed to play without ads (same as in a browser).

While a stream plays, move the mouse over it to show the bar:
  drag the title to move the window, drag the right or bottom edge
  (or the corner) to resize. House = back to channels, pin = always
  on top, then minimize and close.

Right-click the bar (or press the menu button) for color palettes,
opacity, updates, sign-out and the settings folder.

It uses Microsoft Edge WebView2, which Windows 10 and 11 already have.
Your favorites, settings and Twitch sign-in are saved in
%APPDATA%\PhosphorWatch, so updates never touch them.
